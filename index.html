01The problem

Classrooms keep running after students leave

Classroom ACs, tube lights and fans stay on once the last student walks out, and nobody measures how much or for how long.

What problem am I solving?

ACs, tube lights and fans left on in empty classrooms, with no data on how long or how much power is wasted.

Who is affected, and why does it matter?

KCC (about 300 rooms) pays for the electricity, everyone shares the extra carbon, and no one is responsible for switching off.

What inspired me?

Seeing it in my own classrooms during a Campus Sustainability Assessment, and hearing the same concern from a classmate.

02Insights and research

What I found out

Observed on campus

On 8 Oct 2026 I photographed a classroom with AC, lights and fans on and a washroom tap running. A classmate, Pratyaksh Pandey, raised both issues.

03The AI-enabled solution

SwitchSmart

The system switches off the AC, fans and lights when a room is empty and no class is due, and uses AI to spot waste that a fixed rule cannot predict.

1. SENSE

Sensors

Presence, room power and the class timetable.

2. DECIDE

Controller

Empty for 15 minutes and no class due: switch off.

3. LEARN

AI model

Scores how unusual each room's power use is.

4. ACT

Alerts and dashboard

Staff alerts with a reason, and the 10 worst rooms.

Presence sensing

Detects people, not bags, so a room with bags on benches still switches off.

Timetable-aware

Both the timetable and the sensor must agree. A 1-hour override button keeps users in control.

Measured in kWh

Every room is metered, so waste becomes a number a department head can act on.

How AI is used

Isolation Forest anomaly detection learns what normal power use looks like for each room, hour and day, then flags unusual windows. It needs no labelled examples, which KCC does not have yet.

Tools and AI used

Python, pandas and scikit-learn for the simulation. Proposed hardware and software:

Rollout in stages

Pilot: weeks 1 to 8

10 rooms against 10 similar rooms. Goal: 50% less waste and 2 or fewer complaints a month.

Expansion: months 3 to 6

About 100 rooms. Goal: 70% of AI flags confirmed real by staff, and a named owner for alerts.

Full-scale: months 7 to 12

All about 300 rooms, then labs and offices, with a monthly report to the department head.

Main challenges and how I would handle them

The AC cuts off during a lecture

Switch-off needs both the timetable and the presence sensor to agree, there is a 1-hour override button, and the 15-minute wait can be lengthened. Warning sign: complaints rise above 2 a month.

Sensors miss people sitting still

Use presence sensors that detect stationary people (mmWave) and test them in the pilot before buying more.

Alerts are ignored

Name one owner for alerts, track alerts acted on within 24 hours, and have the department head sign off a monthly report.

The budget is not approved

Start with 10 rooms, get vendor quotes, and show the kWh saved and the payback before asking for more.
